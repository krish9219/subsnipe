title: Cancel Netflix
officialUrl: https://www.netflix.com/cancelplan
---

Netflix lets you cancel on the web. You keep the plan until the date you already paid through.

1. Sign in at netflix.com and open Account from the profile menu.
2. Choose Cancel Membership, then Finish Cancellation.
3. Confirm the email. Download any offline titles you still want before the end date.
4. If the button sends you to Apple, Google Play, or a partner such as Airtel or Jio, cancel there instead.

Note: A UPI autopay can keep debiting if the mandate outlives the Netflix cancel. Revoke it in your UPI app after the membership page says the plan will end.
