title: Cancel an Apple subscription
officialUrl: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions
---

Apple TV+, Apple Music, iCloud+, and Apple One are all cancelled from Apple ID subscriptions, not from the individual app.

1. On an iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
2. Or open the Apple subscriptions page on the web and sign in.
3. Select the plan and tap Cancel. Confirm the date it ends.
4. The organizer of a Family Sharing plan has to cancel it. Other family members cannot.

Note: Cancelling iCloud+ does not delete photos immediately, but you must get storage under the free limit or Apple will eventually freeze new uploads. Download anything you cannot lose first.
