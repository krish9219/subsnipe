title: Stop an Airtel autopay
officialUrl: https://www.airtel.in/
---

Airtel postpaid, Black, Xstream, and DTH renewals are stopped in Airtel Thanks or on the Airtel site, and the bank mandate has to be revoked too.

1. Open Airtel Thanks and sign in with the paying number.
2. Go to Manage, then the service that matches the narration (mobile, fiber, DTH, or Black).
3. Turn off autopay or change the plan so it does not renew.
4. Open your UPI app or net banking and revoke the Airtel mandate. Do both. Cancelling only one side often leaves the debit in place.

Note: A prepaid recharge is not always a subscription. If the narration is a one-time recharge you chose, there may be nothing to cancel until an autopay is actually active.
