cult.fit packs are managed in the app. Many packs are paid up front, so cancelling stops renewal rather than refunding sessions already bought.

1. Open the cult.fit app and go to your profile.
2. Open Packs or Membership.
3. Choose the active pack and then Cancel or Do not renew, if that option is offered.
4. If you bought the pack on the Play Store or App Store, cancel it there as well.

Note: A pack that was paid in full usually runs until it expires. The app shows whether a refund is available. Do not expect unused classes to return as cash unless the screen says so.
