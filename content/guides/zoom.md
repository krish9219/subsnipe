title: Cancel Zoom
officialUrl: https://zoom.us/billing
---

Paid Zoom plans are cancelled from the billing page by the account owner.

1. Sign in at zoom.us/billing.
2. Open Current plans and choose Cancel.
3. Confirm. The paid features last until the term ends, then the account returns to Basic.
4. If your employer owns the account, you will not see billing. Ask the owner.

Note: Cancelling does not delete cloud recordings immediately, but Basic accounts have tight cloud limits. Download recordings first.
