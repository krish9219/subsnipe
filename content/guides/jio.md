title: Stop a Jio autopay
officialUrl: https://www.jio.com/selfcare/plans/mobility/my-plans/
---

Jio prepaid, postpaid, and fiber plans are managed in MyJio. Autopay is a separate switch from the plan itself.

1. Open MyJio and go to the number or fiber account that is being billed.
2. Open My plans or Autopay.
3. Switch off autopay or choose a plan that does not renew automatically.
4. Revoke the Jio mandate in your UPI app or bank. Keep a screenshot of the off state.

Note: JioHotstar billed by Jio is a different product from a mobile recharge. If the narration says Hotstar, use the JioHotstar steps. If it says Jio fiber or prepaid, stay in MyJio.
