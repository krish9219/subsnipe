Zomato Gold is cancelled from the membership screen in the Zomato app.

1. Open the app and go to your profile.
2. Open Gold, then membership details.
3. Choose Cancel and confirm.
4. Read the date benefits stop. A trial converts on that date if you do nothing.

Note: Restaurant discounts and the membership fee are separate. Cancelling Gold does not refund individual orders. Revoke any leftover UPI mandate.
