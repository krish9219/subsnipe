officialUrl: https://www.zee5.com/myaccount/subscription
---

Zee5 shows the active plan under My Account once you are signed in.

1. Sign in and open My Account, then My Subscription.
2. Choose Cancel subscription and confirm.
3. Check the end date. Downloads may stop when the plan ends.
4. Store or operator billing has to be cancelled at the source named on the page.

Note: If the bank narration says a mandate, revoke that autopay after Zee5 confirms the plan will not renew.
