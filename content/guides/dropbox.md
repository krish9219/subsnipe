Dropbox Plus and Professional are cancelled from the plan page, not by uninstalling the desktop app.

1. Sign in and open the plan page.
2. Choose Cancel plan and confirm.
3. Check how much storage you will have afterwards and delete or move files that will not fit.
4. A refund, if one applies, is described on the confirmation screen. Do not assume a yearly plan returns the unused months.

Note: Shared folders owned by someone else stay with them. Your copy of those files can disappear from your account when you leave the folder.
