SonyLIV subscriptions are cancelled from the account menu on the site or in the app.

1. Sign in at sonyliv.com or in the app.
2. Open the profile menu and then My Account or Subscription.
3. Choose Cancel and confirm. Note the last day of access.
4. If the charge is from Apple, Google Play, or a telecom pack, cancel it with that provider.

Note: A ₹1 or ₹2 trial charge means the paid plan has not started yet. Cancel before the trial end date shown in the account.
