title: Cancel Tata Play autopay
officialUrl: https://www.tataplay.com/
---

Tata Play (formerly Tata Sky) packs and NACH autopay are managed in the Tata Play account. The bank mandate is cancelled separately.

1. Sign in on the Tata Play site or app and open My Account.
2. Open Manage pack and turn off auto-debit or change the pack.
3. Note the date the current pack runs until. Service usually continues until then.
4. If the statement shows NACH or ECS, also revoke that mandate with your bank so a cancelled pack cannot be debited again.

Note: A pack change is not the same as stopping payment. Read the confirmation to see whether money will still leave your account next cycle.
