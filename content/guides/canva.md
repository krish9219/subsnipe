merchantId: canva
title: Cancel Canva Pro
officialUrl: https://www.canva.com/settings/billing-and-teams
---

Canva Pro is cancelled from billing settings by the owner of the team.

1. Open Canva settings, then Billing.
2. Choose Cancel subscription or Cancel trial.
3. Confirm. Pro features remain until the date shown.
4. If a team owner pays, only that person sees the cancel button.

Note: Download designs you still need. Some Pro assets are replaced or watermarked after the plan ends.
