title: Cancel Swiggy One

---

Swiggy One is cancelled inside the Swiggy account. Food orders and the membership are different charges.

1. Open the Swiggy app and go to Account.
2. Open Swiggy One.
3. Choose Cancel membership and confirm the end date.
4. If you joined with a free trial, cancel before the date the app says the paid plan starts.

Note: After the app confirms cancellation, revoke the UPI autopay in your UPI app if a mandate is still listed. Deleting the Swiggy app does not cancel the plan.
