title: Cancel Google One
officialUrl: https://one.google.com/settings
---

Google One storage is cancelled from the Google One settings page for the account that pays.

1. Open Google One settings while signed in.
2. Choose Cancel membership and read the date storage shrinks.
3. Confirm. If you bought it on an iPhone, Apple's subscription list is the only cancel button that works.
4. Delete or download files until you are under the free quota before the plan ends.

Note: Gmail, Drive, and Photos share one pool. Cancelling does not delete them on the spot, but new mail can fail when you are over the free 15 GB.
