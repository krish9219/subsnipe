JioHotstar (formerly Disney+ Hotstar and JioCinema) is cancelled in the account that bought it, which is often the app rather than a bank.

1. Open JioHotstar and sign in.
2. Open your profile, then Account or Subscription.
3. Choose Cancel subscription and confirm the date access ends.
4. If the plan came with a Jio or Airtel bundle, cancel or change that pack with the operator. The app will say when it does not control billing.

Note: Operator bundles and app-store billing do not stop when you only delete the app. Cancel the source of the charge, then revoke any UPI or NACH mandate still active.
