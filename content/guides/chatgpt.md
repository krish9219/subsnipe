ChatGPT Plus is cancelled in account settings, or in the app store if that is who bills you.

1. Open ChatGPT and go to Settings, then Account.
2. Choose Subscription or Manage my subscription.
3. Select Cancel plan and confirm the end date.
4. If the button opens Apple or Google Play, cancel the subscription there. The ChatGPT site cannot stop a store receipt.

Note: Cancelling stops renewal. It does not delete chats. API credits on platform.openai.com are a separate bill from ChatGPT Plus.
