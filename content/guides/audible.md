Audible memberships are cancelled from account details on the website. Pausing is a different button from cancelling.

1. Sign in at audible.in (or audible.com if that is where you subscribed).
2. Open Account details, then Membership.
3. Choose Cancel membership, not Pause, unless a pause is what you want.
4. Confirm. You can usually keep titles you already used a credit on. Unused credits are described in the confirmation.

Note: Credits do not all survive cancellation. The confirmation screen is the source of truth for which credits remain and for how long.
