Disney+ outside the JioHotstar bundle is cancelled from the Disney+ account subscription page.

1. Sign in at disneyplus.com and open Account, then Subscription.
2. Choose Cancel subscription and confirm.
3. Access continues until the billing date shown.
4. If you subscribed through Apple, Google, or a cable partner, the page will send you to that provider.

Note: In India, Disney+ Hotstar moved to JioHotstar. If your charge says Hotstar or JioHotstar, use that guide instead of this one.
