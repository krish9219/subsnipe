merchantId: spotify
title: Cancel Spotify Premium
officialUrl: https://www.spotify.com/account/subscription/
---

Premium is managed from the Spotify account page on the web, not from the player.

1. Open the subscription page while signed in to the same account that pays.
2. Choose Change plan, then Cancel Premium.
3. Follow the prompts until the page says Premium will end on a specific date.
4. On a phone, the app may only deep-link you to Apple or Google Play. Cancel the store subscription there.

Note: In India, Spotify often bills by UPI autopay. After the site confirms cancellation, check your UPI app for a leftover mandate and revoke it.
