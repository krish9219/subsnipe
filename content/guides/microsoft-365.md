title: Cancel Microsoft 365
officialUrl: https://account.microsoft.com/services
---

Microsoft 365 and many Xbox charges are cancelled from the Microsoft account services page.

1. Sign in at account.microsoft.com/services with the paying account.
2. Find Microsoft 365 and choose Manage, then Cancel.
3. Turn off recurring billing and confirm the end date.
4. If a workplace owns the license, you cannot cancel it. Ask the admin.

Note: A personal subscription can be cancelled and still used until it expires. Save OneDrive files before storage drops to the free tier.
