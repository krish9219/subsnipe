Notion billing sits in Settings for the workspace, and only an admin can cancel it.

1. Open the workspace, then Settings, then Billing.
2. Choose Change plan or Cancel plan.
3. Confirm. The workspace drops to the free plan at the end of the period.
4. If you only wanted to leave a workspace someone else pays for, ask that admin. Cancelling your own login does not stop their card.

Note: Duplicate pages you need onto a free workspace before the paid features turn off.
