title: Cancel Amazon Prime
officialUrl: https://www.amazon.in/gp/primecentral
---

Prime in India is managed from Prime Central. Video, shipping, and Music are one membership.

1. Sign in at amazon.in and open Prime Central.
2. Choose Manage membership, then End membership or Do not renew.
3. Pick a reason and confirm. Amazon often offers a reminder or a smaller plan. You can still finish cancellation.
4. If you joined through an app store, use that store's subscription list instead.

Note: Ending Prime stops the next renewal. It does not refund a yearly plan automatically. The page states whether anything will be returned.
