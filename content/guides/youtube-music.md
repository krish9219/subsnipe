title: Cancel YouTube Music
officialUrl: https://www.youtube.com/paid_memberships
---

A standalone YouTube Music membership is cancelled from the same Google paid-memberships page as YouTube Premium.

1. Sign in with the Google account that pays for Music.
2. Open paid memberships and select YouTube Music.
3. Choose Cancel and confirm the end date.
4. If the charge comes from Apple or a carrier bundle, cancel that provider. Google cannot stop a bill it does not send.

Note: Family or student plans must be cancelled by the person who owns the billing account.
