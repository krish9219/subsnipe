title: Cancel YouTube Premium
officialUrl: https://www.youtube.com/paid_memberships
---

YouTube Premium, including the music benefit, is cancelled from Google's paid memberships page.

1. Open paid memberships while signed in to the Google account that is billed.
2. Select YouTube Premium, then Manage membership.
3. Choose Cancel membership and confirm. Benefits last until the period ends.
4. If you subscribed in the iOS app, cancel under Apple subscriptions instead. Play Store signups are listed at Google Play subscriptions.

Note: Cancelling YouTube Premium also ends YouTube Music Premium when they are one membership. A separate YouTube Music plan has its own row.
