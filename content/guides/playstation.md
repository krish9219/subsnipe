PlayStation Plus is cancelled from account management on the web or on the console. Sony's help page walks through both.

1. Sign in at the PlayStation account site or go to Settings, then Users and Accounts, then Account, then Subscriptions on the console.
2. Select PlayStation Plus.
3. Turn off auto-renew or cancel, and confirm.
4. The plan stays active until the date you paid through. Online multiplayer stops after that.

Note: Games you claimed from the monthly catalog can lock when Plus ends. They become playable again if you resubscribe. Buying a game outright is separate from the subscription.
