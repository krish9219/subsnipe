title: Cancel Truecaller Premium
officialUrl: https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions
---

Truecaller Premium is almost always billed by Google Play or the App Store. Cancelling inside the app only works when it sends you to that store.

1. Open Truecaller, then Settings, then Premium.
2. Choose Manage subscription. It should open Google Play or Apple subscriptions.
3. Cancel the Truecaller row there and confirm the end date.
4. If you cannot find it, open the store's subscription list directly and search for Truecaller.

Note: Uninstalling Truecaller does not cancel the store subscription. The store will keep billing until you cancel it there.
