merchantId: linkedin
title: Cancel LinkedIn Premium
officialUrl: https://www.linkedin.com/psettings/premium-subscription
---

LinkedIn Premium is cancelled from Premium settings on the website. The mobile app often only points you there.

1. Open the Premium subscription settings while signed in.
2. Choose Cancel subscription.
3. Finish every screen until LinkedIn shows the last day of Premium.
4. If you subscribed through an app store, cancel in that store. LinkedIn will say so.

Note: Cancelling Premium does not delete your profile. InMail and seeing-who-viewed-you stop when the period ends.
