title: Cancel Adobe
officialUrl: https://account.adobe.com/plans
---

Adobe plans are cancelled from the account plans page. Annual plans paid monthly can include an early-termination fee.

1. Sign in at account.adobe.com/plans.
2. Open Manage plan on the subscription you want to stop.
3. Choose Cancel your plan and read the fee or refund shown before you confirm.
4. Confirm only after the page shows the amount, if any, that will still be charged.

Note: Adobe's flow often offers a cheaper plan or a pause. Those are optional. The cancellation is finished only when the plan status says it will end.
